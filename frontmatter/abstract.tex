\frontmattertitle{Abstract}

Long-distance bus ticketing in Nepal still depends largely on physical counters, phone calls, and local agents. This causes problems for passengers, who cannot easily check seat availability or compare fares, and for operators, who manage seat records manually and face double booking and disputes. This project proposes the Nepal Bus Ticket Booking System, a web-based application that lets passengers search for buses by origin, destination, and travel date, choose seats from a visual seat map, and book tickets online. Bus operators can manage routes, buses, schedules, and fares, and administrators can oversee users, bookings, and reports.

The system prevents double booking through a temporary seat-locking mechanism. It uses sorting and searching algorithms to present results by price or departure time, a fare calculation module that supports special pricing rules, and reports on bookings, revenue, and seat occupancy. Payment is demonstrated through a simulated workflow that can later be replaced by a real payment gateway. The system will be developed as a responsive web application using Next.js, NestJS, PostgreSQL, and Tailwind CSS.

\textbf{Keywords:} bus ticket booking, seat locking, web application, Nepal, NestJS, PostgreSQL
